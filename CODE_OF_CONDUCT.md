# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want KingCobra to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience level, background,
identity, or the platform they run. We expect the same of everyone who takes part.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback.
- Keep discussion on the technical merits.
- Help newcomers; everyone started somewhere.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow this code. To report
a problem, email **divyanshu.sinha631@gmail.com** with links or screenshots. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
